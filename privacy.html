Personal information is not sold. It may be shared where necessary with email, cloud-storage and IT providers, professional advisers, or public authorities where disclosure is legally required.

If an enquiry becomes client work, relevant information may become part of the client and business records retained for contractual, tax, legal and professional purposes. Records are securely deleted when they are no longer required.

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may have rights to access, correct or erase your information, restrict its use, object to its use, and receive certain information in a portable format.

Your right to object: you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may always object to the use of your information for direct marketing.

If you have a concern, please contact Sheppy Consulting first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
